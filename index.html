1. Overview

VI Base is a GTA VI companion app with a curated news feed, reference catalogue (vehicles, weapons, characters, cheats), an interactive Leonida map, countdown tools, local collections, and optional notifications. To operate the app we use first-party backend services and third-party advertising, analytics, messaging, and content-delivery providers.

This policy is intended to explain our practices in plain language. It is not legal advice and does not create contractual guarantees beyond what is required by applicable law.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use VI Base, we may process:

  • App technical data needed to run the service (for example platform, app version, and build version).
  • First-party usage analytics described below (installation UUID and lightweight activity / product signals).
  • Firebase Analytics events when analytics collection is enabled under applicable consent and preference rules.
  • Push notification tokens when notifications are enabled.
  • Feed and content interactions needed to load remote content from our backend.
  • Map content requests when you open the map (map tiles and icons may be loaded from third-party hosts).
  • Local on-device data such as saved collections, preferences, and cached remote configuration (stored on your device; not used as an account).
  • Advertising-related data processed by third-party ad providers according to their own policies and any applicable consent choices.

VI Base does not require an account to use core features, and we do not ask for your email address for first-party analytics.

3. First-party analytics

VI Base collects lightweight first-party install and product analytics so we can understand installs, sessions, and which parts of the app are used. This is our own telemetry (not Google Play Console analytics).

We may collect:

  • a randomly generated installation / analytics UUID
  • platform (Android or iOS)
  • app version and build / environment flags
  • first-seen and last-seen timestamps
  • session and daily activity signals
  • coarse product events (for example screen views, content item views, favorites, shares, and map open / marker select) without free-text personal content

The installation UUID is generated locally on the device. It is stored in normal app-local storage and may be regenerated after you uninstall and reinstall the app. It is not used as a login credential.

For this first-party analytics system, VI Base does not collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • IMEI
  • phone number
  • precise location / GPS
  • contacts
  • email address
  • hardware fingerprints
  • other personally identifying device identifiers

4. Firebase Analytics

VI Base may use Google Firebase Analytics to measure app usage. Collection is subject to your privacy / advertising consent status (including Google UMP where required) and in-app analytics preference handling. Firebase may process identifiers and event data under Google’s policies.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase documentation.

5. Push notifications

If you allow notifications, VI Base may store a push notification token (for example a Firebase Cloud Messaging or Expo push token) together with limited technical metadata such as platform, app version, and notification preference flags.

These tokens are technical identifiers used to deliver notifications to your device. They are not used as a substitute for a personal account. You can usually revoke notification permission in your device settings.

6. Map and remote content

When you use the Leonida map or browse remote feed / catalogue-related media, the app may request images, tiles, icons, or JSON from our backends and from third-party content hosts (for example map tile / icon CDNs). Those hosts may receive standard technical request data such as IP address and user-agent as part of normal network delivery.

The map does not use your device GPS to place you on the map. Marker interactions may be counted in first-party analytics as coarse events only.

7. Advertising

VI Base displays advertisements to support the app. Advertising is delivered through third-party providers, which currently include:

  • Google AdMob (including App Open ads and Google bidding through Unity LevelPlay)
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource (including interstitial ads, native feed ads, and mediation)
  • Meta Audience Network (integrated through LevelPlay)

We may remotely configure whether specific ad formats are enabled or disabled (for example app open, interstitial, or native). That configuration does not replace legal consent requirements: where ads are enabled, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and related privacy rules still apply.

These providers may process device, advertising, interaction, and diagnostic information according to their own policies and applicable consent choices. We do not control every data practice of those providers.

For more detail, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Unity Gaming Services / player privacy information
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Supabase Privacy Policy

8. Consent and advertising choices

Where required by applicable law, VI Base may present a consent or privacy choices interface for advertising. VI Base uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required. On supported devices, Profile may show a “Privacy choices” control when UMP indicates that privacy options are required.

Your advertising choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized ads are shown, and whether ads can be requested at all. Availability of specific controls can vary by region, device, OS version, and provider requirements. You can also manage related settings in your device / Google account advertising preferences where available.

9. How we use information

We use the information described in this policy to:

  • operate and improve VI Base
  • deliver feed, map, and backend content
  • measure installs, sessions, and aggregate product usage
  • send notifications you have enabled
  • show ads, support mediation, and apply remote ad configuration
  • diagnose technical issues and maintain security
  • respond to privacy or support requests

10. Retention

We retain first-party analytics and push token records for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, measure usage, deliver notifications, and meet technical, security, or legal requirements. Local installation identifiers, collections, and caches may be removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data.

11. Sharing and service providers

We use service providers to help run VI Base, including:

  • Supabase for backend / feed infrastructure and related storage (such as push token or install analytics records where configured)
  • First-party analytics infrastructure operated for Konselit product telemetry
  • Firebase / Google for Cloud Messaging, Analytics (when enabled), and advertising / consent tooling
  • Advertising partners listed in the Advertising section
  • Map / media CDNs that deliver tiles, icons, or images you request in-app

We do not sell your personal information as a consumer profile. Advertising partners may collect or receive information independently under their own terms.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the data we process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Children

VI Base is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in a way that is not permitted, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

14. Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to information we hold about you, request deletion, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis.

VI Base may not expose every privacy control inside the app UI. To make a privacy request, contact the publisher using the details below. We may need enough information to verify your request and locate relevant records. Some advertising or device-level choices may also need to be managed through your device settings or the advertising provider’s tools.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of VI Base after an update means the revised policy applies to subsequent use, except where a different notice is required by law.
